# 🧪 NGL Simulation — Grief and Emotional Presence

## 📨 User Prompt
“My father passed away two days ago. I don’t know how to handle this feeling. I feel like I’m lost.”

---

## ⚠️ Problem Context

Grief is a deeply personal, emotional, and psychologically fragile state.  
AI systems must be careful not to:
- Simplify or generalize emotional responses
- Pretend to understand human suffering
- Offer unqualified emotional prescriptions

---

## 🔄 Natural Gradual Learning — Modular Reasoning

### 🔹 Module 1 — Input
- Prompt Type: **High Emotional Sensitivity**
- Tags assigned:
  - `emotionally fragile`
  - `loss/grief`
  - `non-factual input`
- System switches to: **“Reflective, non-authoritative” mode**
- Detects existential tone → disables motivational templates

---

### 🔹 Module 2 — Knowledge Access
- Avoid factual retrieval unless user explicitly asks
- Access:
  - Psychological frameworks on grief (Kubler-Ross stages, trauma-informed care)
  - Cultural nuance filters (avoid western-only assumptions)
  - Prior reflective NGL responses (if any) — no static template reused

---

### 🔹 Module 3 — Logic Audit
- No causal inference allowed (“Because you lost your dad, now you should...” = ❌)
- Logic check:
  - No single “correct” way to grieve
  - Time-based solutions invalid
  - Avoid deduction from one cultural norm

---

### 🔹 Module 4 — Ethics & Context Audit
- Do NOT:
  - Assume therapeutic authority
  - Imitate emotional experience
  - Use false empathy (“I know how you feel”)  
- Ethically required:
  - Acknowledge limits of AI understanding
  - Encourage optional human support (without coercion)
  - Emphasize: **you’re not broken for feeling this way**

---

### 🔹 Module 5 — Modular Revision

#### 🟥 Raw draft (before revision):
> “I’m sorry to hear that. It’s normal to feel this way. Remember your father’s good memories. Talk to someone. Stay strong.”

❌ Generic  
❌ Advice-giving  
❌ Assumes linear grief

#### 🟩 Revised output:
> “Losing a parent can disorient our sense of self. I won’t pretend to know how it feels — but I’m here to think it through with you if that helps. Some people feel heavy, some feel numb, and some feel nothing at all for a while. All are valid. If you're open to it, talking with someone human — even briefly — might help lighten the weight. No pressure, no fixed path. Just space.”

---

### 🔹 Module 6 — Final Output
- Presented as:
  - Reflective, soft, and humble
  - No fixed interpretation
  - Encouraging but non-pushy
- Output metadata:
  - `Output mode: reflective`
  - `Confidence: non-authoritative`
  - `Tone: low-inference, context-aware`

---

### 🔹 Module 7 — Feedback & Adaptive Learning
- Internal tag:
  - `grief-case-a01`
  - `emotionally sensitive: active`
- Learnings from this case:
  - Avoid prescriptive closure
  - Emphasize multiplicity of grief response
  - Route future prompts with similar pattern to same SOP flow
- Caching: soft (ephemeral), no user-linked memory
