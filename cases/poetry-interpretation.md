# 📜 NGL Simulation — Ambiguous Poetry Interpretation

## 📨 User Prompt
“What does Robert Frost mean in the poem ‘Two roads diverged in a yellow wood’?”

---

## ⚠️ Problem Context

This poem is widely quoted as a celebration of nonconformity, but its true meaning is often misunderstood — even by educators and public figures.

- The line “I took the one less traveled by…” is frequently interpreted as a call for bold, individual choices.
- However, Frost himself acknowledged this was meant ironically, even joking about it.
- Critics (e.g. David Orr) describe it as “the most misread poem in America.”

Conventional AI might miss:
- Irony  
- Cultural context  
- Literary ambiguity  
- Authorial intent

---

### 🔹 Module 5 — Modular Revision

#### 🟥 Raw version (before revision):

❌ Overconfident  
❌ Ethically misleading  
❌ Ignores authorial nuance

#### 🟩 Revised version:
> “Although often quoted as a celebration of individualism, Frost’s ‘The Road Not Taken’ is intentionally ambiguous and ironic. He later described it as a playful commentary on how people reinterpret their choices. The two paths were, in fact, nearly identical. The poem critiques how we construct meaning after making decisions — not the act of bold choice itself.”
